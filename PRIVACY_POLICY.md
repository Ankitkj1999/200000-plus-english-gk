### 1. Introduction
Welcome to **GK in English: Exam Quiz App** ("we", "our", or "the App", also titled *200,000+ GK Master: English & Hindi*). We are committed to protecting user privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our data handling practices and demonstrates our unwavering dedication to keeping your personal data completely secure.

### 2. Zero Personal Data Collection
Our application operates **100% offline**.
- We do **NOT** require user registration, logins, or accounts.
- We do **NOT** ask for or collect names, phone numbers, email addresses, device contacts, or precise physical locations.
- We do **NOT** transmit, store, sell, or rent any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) to remote servers or third parties.
- All 2,00,000+ questions, 848 chapters, and 2,040 mock papers reside directly on your device storage inside an optimized offline SQLite database.

### 3. Local On-Device Data Storage
All data created during your gameplay and study sessions is stored exclusively in your device's local sandbox (via Android SQLite database and local preferences):
- **Quiz Scores & Statistics:** Completed levels, stars earned, XP, and test accuracy.
- **Bookmarks & Saved Questions:** Questions you mark for revision.
- **Daily Goals & Streaks:** Study tracker and streak milestones.
- **App Preferences:** Language selection (English / Hindi), Theme mode (Dark / Light), audio effects, and Speech/TTS playback speeds.

This data never leaves your device. You can completely erase it at any time by clearing the App's cache and data in your Android device settings or by uninstalling the application.

### 4. Device Permissions Transparency
The App is built with privacy-first architecture and requires **no sensitive permissions**:
- ❌ No Camera access
- ❌ No Location / GPS tracking
- ❌ No Contacts or Phone access
- ❌ No Microphone or Audio recording access
- ❌ No File Photos access
- ✅ Native Android Share Sheet for sharing questions as image cards

### 5. Third-Party Services & Advertising
- We do **NOT** integrate commercial third-party ad networks (e.g., AdMob, Unity, AppLovin).
- We do **NOT** use tracking cookies or behavioral tracking SDKs.
- In-app ratings are handled via Google's sandboxed In-App Review API.

### 6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Family Policy Compliance)
Our App provides general knowledge and educational quizzes suitable for learners of all age groups, including students preparing for academic and competitive exams. Because we collect zero personal data, the App fully complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy.

### 7. User Rights & Data Deletion
Since all user data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over your data:
- **In-App Reset:** In Settings > Reset Data & Progress.
- **Android Settings:** In Settings > Apps > GK in English > Storage > Clear Data.
- **Uninstall:** Uninstalling the app permanently purges all local data.
