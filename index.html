1 Introduction

Welcome to GK in English: Exam Quiz App (referred to as "we", "our", or "the App", also titled 200,000+ GK Master: English & Hindi). We provide self-paced, completely offline general knowledge, subject-wise chapter studies, and competitive exam preparation quizzes for candidates across India (SSC CGL/CHSL/MTS, RRB NTPC/Group D, UPSC, Railway, Banking, Police, State PSCs, and Teaching exams) in both English and Hindi.

This Privacy Policy explains how our application operates, what on-device information is stored, and confirms our strict commitment to safeguarding user privacy in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and applicable data protection regulations.

2 Zero Personal Data Collection

We respect your privacy completely. When using our App:

  • No Account Required: You never need to sign up, log in, or provide your name, phone number, email address, password, or physical location.
  • No Personal Tracking: We do not collect, monitor, sell, or rent your personal data to advertisers, data brokers, or any commercial entities.
  • No Cloud Sync Requirement: All 2,00,000+ questions, 848 chapters, and 2,040 mock papers reside directly on your device storage inside an optimized offline SQLite database.

3 On-Device Data Storage & User Preferences

Any study progress you make is stored strictly in your device's local sandbox (using Android SQLite database and local AsyncStorage preferences):

  • Quiz Progress & Scores: Completed level indexes, stars earned, total XP, and test accuracy rates.
  • Bookmarks: Questions you tap to save for revision and practice.
  • Study Habits & Streaks: Daily study streak counter and daily question goals.
  • Application Preferences: Language selection (English / Hindi), Theme mode (Dark / Light), audio/TTS speech rate and pitch settings.

This data never leaves your smartphone. You maintain 100% ownership and control over it at all times.

5 Third-Party Services, Ads & Trackers

We believe learning should be clean, focused, and uninterrupted:

  • No Third-Party Advertising: The application does not integrate commercial ad network SDKs (such as AdMob, Unity, or AppLovin).
  • No Cross-App Trackers: We do not track your activity across other applications or websites.
  • Google Play In-App Review: When requesting a review, the app uses Google's official, sandboxed In-App Review API, which operates under Google's standard security and privacy guarantees.

6 Children's Privacy (COPPA & Family Policy Compliance)

Our educational content is universally accessible and suitable for students of all age groups, including minors and school learners preparing for trivia or scholarship tests.

Because the app does not collect any personally identifiable information, it is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy requirements. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under 13.

7 Data Deletion & User Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete your data at any time through standard, transparent methods:

  • In-App Reset: Go to Settings > Reset Data & Progress to clear your completed levels and scores.
  • Android App Settings: Go to Device Settings > Apps > GK in English > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data.
  • Uninstall: Uninstalling the application immediately and permanently removes all locally stored scores, bookmarks, and preferences.
